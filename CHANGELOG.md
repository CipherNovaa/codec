# Changelog

Every codec release has a one-line name. The recent ones in full; the earlier
history by name. Dates are when the release was tagged.

## v1.49.0 — two ways into YouTube, and a page for every video · 2026-10-18
- YouTube through a browser's existing login (every Firefox- and Chrome-family profile found and named), or **codec's own lists** with no account: follows, Watch Later, playlists, history, a FOR YOU of its own, import from an account or a Google Takeout
- Every video's own page (`i`): channel, likes against Return YouTube Dislike, description, CHAPTERS · COMMENTS (read, liked, written) · RELATED
- Channel and playlist headers: VIDEOS · SHORTS · LIVE · PLAYLISTS, PLAY ALL · SHUFFLE · PIN AS A TAB
- SUBS gets ALL CHANNELS and NOTIFICATIONS (with CLEAR ALL); select mode (`v`) acts on many rows at once; search remembers and suggests

## v1.48.0 — anyone's machine, a hub with a lock, a laptop with a key · 2026-10-16
- A new install asks **CHOOSE MEDIA PATHS** (codec's own folder browser, MOVIES or TV per path) and wears codec's look by default
- Art with no key of your own; scripts found wherever codec lives; data moved to `~/.local/share/codec`
- The hub **locks its door**: a foreign web page gets media only, a rebinding Host is refused, devices pair with "WANTS TO JOIN · ⏎ ALLOW"
- **SHARE THIS LIBRARY** writes the background service and announces the hub; **USE A CODEC HUB** joins one
- The Cast receiver exports as one static page anyone's TV can load
- Builds and runs on Fedora 41 and Debian 13
